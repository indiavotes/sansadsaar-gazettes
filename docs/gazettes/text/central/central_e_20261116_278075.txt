New Delhi, the l3th October, 2026 
F. No. PROJ-503/6/2026-PIU Chandigarh E-30737.— Whereas, the Mohali-Sirhind Section of National 
Highway-205AG starting from its junction with NH-205A near Sector-!l0 and 09, SAS Nagar (Km. 0+000) 
connecting Districts SAS Nagar and Sirhind in the State of Punjab and terminating at its junction with NH-44 near 


village Bhatmajra (Km. 27+370) is a 4/6 Lane 27.370 Km. (Km. 0+000 to Km. 27+370) long Access Controlled 
Greenfield Highway; 


And whereas there have been and are alternative routes and roads available to the public for linking the above 


places/accessing various destination points before the development of this Highway; 


And whereas this National Highway has been developed as a high-speed corridor and the maximum speed 
limits for Motor Vehicles of various descriptions for the National Highway is 00 km/hour as per the Gazette 
Notification S.O. 522(E) dated 6th April, 208; 


And whereas the movement of high-speed vehicles may pose risk to the safety of certain classes of 
comparatively slow-moving vehicles e.g., two wheelers, three wheelers and other slow-moving vehicles like 
non-motorized vehicles, agricultural tractors (with or without trailers) due to their vulnerability and associated speed 


differentials and compromise the road safety aspects;
