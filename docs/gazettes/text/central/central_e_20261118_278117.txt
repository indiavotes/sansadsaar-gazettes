99.0. 6286(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20A of the 
Railways(Amendment) Act, l989(24/89) (hereinafter referred as the said Act.), the Central Government, after being

out grounds thereof and competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or 
through a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further inquiry, if any, as 
the competent authority thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of 20D of the said Act shall be final. The 
land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected by the interested 
person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
