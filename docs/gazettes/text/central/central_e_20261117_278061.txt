NOTIFICATION 
Maligaon, the l3th November, 2026 
5.0. 6249(E).— Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in Ministry of Railways [N.F. 
Railway (Construction Department)], Number S.O. 4240 (E) Dtd. 29.07.2026 published on 30.07.2026 in the Gazette 
of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) and issued under Sub-section (l) of section 20A of The 
Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008 (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government, declared its 
intention to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed to the said notification for the public purpose of 
execution, maintenance, management and operation of the Special Railway Project namely, "Buniadpur-Kaliyaganj 


New BG Railway Line Project" in the District of Dakshin Dinajpur in the State of West Bengal; 


And whereas, the substance of the said notification has been published in the daily newspapers, namely The 
Telegraph (English Edition), Dtd. 7.08.2026 and Uttarbanga Sambad (Bengali Edition) Dtd. 7.08.2026 under Sub- 
Section (4) of section — 20A of the said Act; 


And whereas, no objection has been received by the competent Authority; 


And whereas, in pursuance of Sub-Section (.) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Competent Authority has 


submitted its report to the Central Government; 


And, therefore, upon receipt of the said report of the competent authority and in exercise of the power conferred 
by Sub-Section (l) of Section 20E of the said act, the Central Government hereby declares that the land specified in the 


Scheduled annexed to this notification shall be acquired for the aforesaid purpose; 


And, further, in pursuance of Sub-Section (2) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that on publication of this notification in the Official Gazette, the land specified in the Schedule Annexed to 


this notification shall vest absolutely in the Central Government free from all encumbrances.
