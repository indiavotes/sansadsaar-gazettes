S.R.O. 32.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309 of the Constitution and in 


supersession of the Border Roads Engineering Service Group 'A' Rules, 977, in so far as they relates to the posts to 
be manned by Border Roads Engineering Service officers, except as respects things done or omitted to be done before 
such supersession, the President hereby makes the following rules regulating the method of recruitment to the Border 
Roads Engineering Service (Group 'A') in the Ministry of Defence, namely:-

Short title and commencement.- (!) These rules may be called the Border Roads Engineering Service (Group

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
Definitions.- In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires, 


"cadre" means the Civil Engineering Cadre or Electrical and Mechanical Engineering Cadre, as the case may 
be and as specified in the First Schedule appended to these rules; 


"cadre controlling authority" means the Ministry of Defence, Government of India; 
"Commission" means the Union Public Service Commission; 


"departmental candidate" means a person who has been appointed on regular basis after consultation with the 
Commission or promoted on the recommendations of the Departmental Promotion Committee; 


“Departmental Promotion Committee, Departmental Confirmation Committee and Departmental Screening 
Committee” means Committees constituted to consider promotion, confirmation, grant of Non-Functional 
Upgradation and Non-Functional Second Grade respectively in any grade as indicated in the Fourth Schedule 
and Fifth Schedule appended to these rules; 


"duty post" means any post specified in column (2) of First Schedule appended to these rules; 


"examination" means Engineering Service Examination conducted by the Commission for recruitment to 
various Engineering Services or posts as may be notified by the Government from time to time; 


"Government" means the Government of India; 
"grade" means a grade of service specified in column (2) of the First Schedule appended to these rules; 


“regular service” in relation to any grade means the period of service in the grade rendered after selection 
and regular appointment to that grade under the rules and includes any period- 


(i) taken into account for purpose of seniority in the case of those already in service at the time of 
notification of these rules; 


(ii) during which an officer would have held a duty post in the grade but for being on leave or otherwise 
not being available for holding such post; 


"Schedule" means a Schedule appended to these rules; 


"Service" means the Border Roads Engineering (Group 'A’) Service constituted in accordance with the 
provision of rule 3 of these rules. 


Constitution of Border Roads Engineering (Group 'A') Service.- The service known as the Border Roads 
Engineering (Group 'A') Service shall consist of posts specified in the First Schedule. 


Grades, authorised strength and its review.- (|) The designations, levels in the pay matrix, grades, sanctioned 
strength of the duty posts, on the date of commencement of these rules, shall be as specified in the First 
Schedule.

5. Members of the Service.- () The following persons shall be members of the Service, namely:- 
(i) Persons appointed to Service under rule 6; and 
(ii) Persons appointed to the Service under rule 7. 


(5) | A person appointed under sub-rule (l) above shall become a member of the Service in the appropriate grade 
applicable to him from the date of such appointment under these rules. 


6. Initial constitution of the Service.- (.) The Service shall consist of the following persons, namely:- 


(i) the persons already appointed on a regular basis to a duty post of Border Roads Engineering (Group 
‘A’) Service on or before the commencement of these rules; 


(ii) the persons appointed to the Service after such commencement. 


(2) For the persons referred to in sub-rule (l) above, regular service rendered prior to the commencement of these 
rules shall continue to 96 counted as regular service for the purpose of seniority, confirmation, | promotion, 
non-functional upgradation, non- functional second grade, pension (if applicable) etc. under these rules. 


(3) All the duty posts in the Service, as specified in the First Schedule, shall be classified as Group ‘A’ 
posts. 


7. Future maintenance of the Service.- () The vacancies in any of the grade shall be filled in the following 
manner, namely:- 


(i) Fifty per cent of the vacancies in the grade of Junior Time Scale shall be filled by direct recruitment 
on the basis of Engineering Service Examination conducted by the Commission in accordance with 
rules made by the Government from time to time, on the basis of the educational qualifications and 
age-limit specified in the Second Schedule; and 


(ii) The remaining fifty per cent of the vacancies in the Junior Time Scale shall be filled by promotion, in 
accordance with the provisions specified in the Third Schedule, in consultation with the Commission 
on the basis of the recommendations of the Departmental Promotion Committee constituted in 
accordance with the Fourth Schedule. 


(2) Appointment in the Service to the posts of Senior Time Scale and above (except for non-functional second 
grade) shall be made in the manner specified in column (3) of the Third Schedule from amongst the members 
of the Service of the next lower grade with the minimum qualifying service as specified in column (5) of the 
Third Schedule. 


(3) Appointment of officers to non-functional second grade (Level-!2) shall be made by placement on the basis 
of seniority as specified in Column (4) of Third Schedule-A. 


(4) All the promotions shall be made by Selection on the basis of the recommendations of the Departmental 
Promotion Committee constituted in accordance with the Fourth Schedule. 


(5) Placements of officers to non-functional second grade shall be made on the basis of seniority, subject to 
suitability on the recommendation of the Committee constituted in accordance with the Fifth Schedule. 


8. Seniority.- (!) The relative seniority of members of the Service appointed or holding any grade, on the date 
of commencement of these rules, shall be same, as determined prior to such commencement; 


(2) If the seniority of any such member has not been determined prior to such commencement, the same 
shall be determined by the cadre controlling authority in accordance with the general instructions issued by 
the Government from time to time.

Probation.- (!) Every member of the Service, appointed to the post of Junior Time Scale of the Service, shall 
be on probation for a period of two years: 


Provided that the cadre controlling authority may extend the period of probation in accordance with 
the instructions or orders issued by the Government from time to time in this regard: 


Provided further that any decision for extension of the period of probation shall be taken ordinarily 
within a period of eight weeks after the expiry of the previous period of probation and the decision in writing 
shall be communicated to the concerned officer together with reasons for so doing within the said period. 


On completion of the period of probation or any extension thereof, officers shall, if considered fit for regular 
appointment, retained in their appointment on regular basis and be confirmed in due course. 


If, during the period of probation referred to in sub-rule (/) or any extension thereof, as the case may be, the 
Government is of the opinion that an officer is not fit for retention or if, at any time during such period of 
probation or extension thereof, the Government is satisfied that the officer will not be fit for permanent 
appointment of the expiration of such period of probation or extension thereof, the Government may for 
reasons to be recorded in writing, discharge him from the Service or revert him to the post which he has been 
holding before his promotion, as the case may be, or pass such orders as it may deem fit. 


During the period of probation or any extension thereof, the officer may be required by the Government to 
undergo such courses of training instructions and to pass such examinations and tests (including an 
examination in Hindi) as it may deem fit for the purpose of satisfactory completion of period of probation. 


As regards other matters relating to the period of probation, the members of the Service shall be governed by 
instructions or orders issued by the Government from time to time in this regard.

. Disqualification :- No person,- 


(a) who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living, or 


(b) who, having a spouse living, has entered into or contracted a marriage with any person,

Service in various grades (upto Higher Administrative Grade) of the Service on the recommendations of

(i) in respect of directly recruited members of the Service in the entry grade (Junior Time Scale), the

3. Power to relax. - Where the Government is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do, it may, by 
order and for reasons to be recorded in writing and in consultation with the Commission, relax any of the 
provisions of these rules with respect to any class or category of persons. 


4. Savings — Nothing in these rules shall affect reservations, relaxation of age-limit and other concessions 
required to be provided for the person belonging to the Scheduled Castes, the Scheduled Tribes, Other 
Backward Classes, Ex-Servicemen and other special categories of person in accordance with the orders 
issued by the Government from time to time in this regard. 


5. Interpretation — If any question arises relating to the interpretation of these rules, it shall be decided by the 
Government.
