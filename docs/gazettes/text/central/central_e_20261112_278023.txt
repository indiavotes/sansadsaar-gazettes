NOTIFICATION 
Secunderabad, the 9th November, 2026 


S.O. 6181(E).— Whereas, by the notification by Government of India, in the Ministry of Railways (Railway 
Board), number S.O.No.3646 (E). Dated.03.07.2026 Published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-I Section 3 
Sub section (1), dated. 04.07.2026 (herein after referred to as said notification) and issued under sub section (1) of 
section 20(A) of the Railway Act, 1989 (24 of 1989), the Central Government declares its intention to acquire the land 
specified in the schedule annexed to the said notification for the Construction of Road Over Bridge in Lieu of Level 
Crossing (LC) No.250 at Km. 607/1-4 in between Bolaram — Cavalry Barracks stations in Machabollaram Village of 
Alwal Mandal in Medchal Malkajgiri District in the State of Telangana. 


And whereas an extract of this notification was published in the daily newspapers The Times of India, Sakshi 
Daily and V6Velugu Newspapers on 09 July, 2026 with the approval of Manoj Kumar, Chief Engineer, Construction, 
Road Safety Projects, South Central Railway, Secunderabad, under sub -section (1) Section 20A of this Act. 


And whereas the Competent Authority for Land Acquisition (CALA) and Revenue Divisional 
Officer, Malkajgiri has reported that no objections have been received from any person interested in the land at 
Machabollaram Village, Alwal Mandal, Medchal—Malkajgiri District, Telangana State, within the stipulated statutory 
period of thirty days as prescribed under Section 20D of the said Act. 


And whereas, in pursuance of sub-section (1) of 20(E) of this Act, the Competent Authority has submitted its 
report to the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, on receipt of the report of the Competent Authority for Land Acquisition (CALA) and in 
exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 20E of the said Act, the Central Government hereby 
declares that the lands mentioned in the Schedule hereto shall be acquired for the purpose mentioned above. 


Further, in pursuance of sub-section (2) of section 20E of this Act, the Central Government hereby declares on 
publication of notification in the Official Gazette, that the land specified in the schedule annexed here to shall vest 
absolutely in the Central Government is free from all encumbrances.
