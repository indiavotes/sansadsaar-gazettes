S.O. 676(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (l) of section 20A of the Railways Act, 
989 (Railways Amendment Act, 2008), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for a public purpose, the

Nagar Tehsils of Rajnandgaon District in the State of Chhattisgarh. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within a period of thirty (30) days from the date of publication of 
this notification in the Official Gazette, object to the acquisition of such land for the aforementioned purpose under sub- 
section (l) of section 20D of the said Act. Every such objection shall be made in writing to the Competent Authority 
and Sub-Divisional Officer (Revenue), Dongargarh, District Rajnandgaon (Chhattisgarh), and shall set forth the grounds 
thereof. The Competent Authority shall provide the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or through 
a legal practitioner (Advocate), and after hearing all such objections and making such further inquiry, if any, as the 


Competent Authority thinks necessary, may, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the Competent Authority under sub-section (2) of section 20D of the said Act shall be 


final. 


The plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected by 


the interested persons at the aforementioned office of the Competent Authority on any working day.
