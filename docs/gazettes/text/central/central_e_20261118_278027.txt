1986 की धारा 3 की उपधारा (1) और उपधारा (2) के खंड (५) के अधीन प्रदत्त शक्तियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, अधिसूचना 
की अनुसूची में सम्मिलित कतिपय प्रवर्गों की परियोजनाओं के लिए पूर्व पर्यावरणीय अनापत्ति को अनिवार्य करने हेतु, संख्या 
का.आ. 1533(अ), तारीख 14 सितम्बर, 2006 द्वारा पर्यावरण समाघात निर्धारण अधिसूचना, 2006 (जिसे इसमें इसके 
पश्चात्‌ उक्त अधिसूचना कहा गया है) प्रकाशित की है; 


और, राज्य स्तरीय पर्यावरण समाघात निर्धारण प्राधिकरणों का गठन पर्यावरण (संरक्षण) अधिनियम, 1986 की 
धारा 3 की उपधारा (3) के अधीन, राज्य स्तर पर पर्यावरण समाघात निर्धारण अधिसूचना, 2006 के कार्यान्वयन तथा प्रवर्ग 
'ख' के अधीन आने वाले सभी प्रस्तावों पर विचार करने और पर्यावरणीय अनापत्ति प्रदान करने के लिए प्रत्यायोजित शक्तियों 
का प्रयोग करने के प्रयोजनार्थ किया गया है;

MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 18th November, 2026 


99.0. 6278(E).— WHEREAS, the Central Government in the erstwhile Ministry of Environment and Forests, 
in exercise of its powers under sub-section (1) and clause (v) of sub-section (2) of section 3 of the Environment 
(Protection) Act, 1986 has published the Environment Impact Assessment Notification, 2006 (hereinafter referred to 


as the said notification), vide number S.O.1533 (E), dated the 14th September, 2006 for mandating prior 
environmental clearance for certain category of projects covered in the schedule of the notification; 


And whereas, the State Level Environment Impact Assessment Authorities have been constituted under sub- 
section (3) of section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 for implementation of the Environment Impact 
Assessment Notification, 2006 at State level for exercising delegated powers to consider and grant environmental 
clearance for all proposals under Category “B’; 


And whereas, the State Level Environment Impact Assessment Authorities have gained substantial 
experience over the past two decades in the environmental clearance appraisal process and the process at the State 
level has also been made completely online through the PARIVESH portal for efficient and transparent disposal of EC 
proposals. Further, the Central Government also constitutes additional State Level Expert Appraisal Committees to 
assist the State Level Environment Impact Assessment Authorities for granting environmental clearances; 


And whereas, the eligibility criteria such as qualifications and experience etc., for experts nominated in the 
Expert Appraisal Committees and State Level Expert Appraisal Committees are identical and are governed by the 
Appendix VI of the Environment Impact Assessment Notification, 2006. In this regard, it is a fact that equal level of 
expertise is available in the State Level Expert Appraisal Committees as compared to the Expert Appraisal Committees; 


And whereas, the States have already been delegated powers for appraising 500 ha., of coal mining projects. 
The impact on environment of non-coal mining projects is less as compared to coal mining. In this regard, the Central 
Government deems it necessary to further decentralise the environmental clearance process with regard to non-coal 
mining projects, by raising the threshold of delegation from 250 ha. to 500 ha., as done in coal mining projects, for 
facilitating clearances at State level; 


And whereas, the recommendations of the Expert Advisory Committee were also sought. Based on the 
recommendations of the Expert Advisory Committee, a draft notification for making amendments in the said 
notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (1), vide number 8.0. 
4166(E) dated 27" July, 2026, inviting objections and suggestions from all the persons likely to be affected thereby, 
within a period of sixty days from the date on which copies of the Gazette containing the said draft notification were 
made available to the public and the objections and suggestions received in response to the said draft notification 
within the period of sixty days have been duly considered by the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) and clause (v) of sub-section (2) of section 
3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 (29 of 1986), read with clause (d) of sub-rule (3) of rule 5 of the 
Environment (Protection) Rules, 1986, the Central Government hereby makes the following further amendments in 
the notification of the Government of India in the erstwhile Ministry of Environment and Forests, vide number 
S.0.1533(E) dated the 14th September, 2006, namely:- 


In the said notification, in the Schedule, against item 1(a),— 
(a) in column (3), for the figures and letters “>250 ha’, the figures and letters “> 500 ha” shall be substituted; 
(b) in column (4), for the figures and letters “< 250 ha’, the figures and letters “ <500 ha” shall be substituted.
