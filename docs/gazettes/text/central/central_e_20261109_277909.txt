Bhubaneswar, the 9th November, 2026 
Notice under Section 20A of the Railways Act-,l989 


S.O. 6(E).— In exercise of powers conferred by sub-section () of section 20A of the Railways (Amendment) 
Act, l989 (24/89) (hereinafter referred as the said Act.), the Central Government, after being satisfied that for the public 
purpose, the Land, brief description of which is given in the schedule annexed here to ,is required for the execution 
Special Railway Project, namely, “Connection with Construction Of Doubling Of Haridaspur-Paradeep BG 
Railway Line(74.08 Km)”: in the district of Kendrapada in the state of Odisha, hereby declares its intention to acquire 
such land. 


Any person interested in the said land may within a period of thirty days from the date of publication of the 
notification in the Official Gazette, raise objection to the acquisition of such land for the aforesaid purpose under sub- 
section (l) of section 20D of the said Act. 


Every objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely. Spl. Land Acquisition Officer, 
Haridaspur-Paradeep Doubling Of BG Rail Line,Kendrapada in writing, and shall set out grounds thereof and 
competent authority shall give the objector an opportunity of being heard, either in person or through a legal practitioner, 
and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further inquiry, if any, as the competent authority 
thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (2) of 20D of the said Act shall be final. 


The land plans and other details of the land covered under this notification are available and can be inspected 
by the interested person at the aforesaid office of the competent authority.
