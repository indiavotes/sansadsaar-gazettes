Short title and commencement.— (1) These rules may be called the National Sports Board (Appointment, 
Salary, Allowances and other Terms and Conditions of Service of Officers and other Employees) Rules, 2026. 


They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 

Definitions.— (1) In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires, — 

“Act” means the National Sports Governance Act, 2025 (25 of 2025); 

“Board” means the National Sports Board established under sub-section (1) of section 5 of the Act; 
“Chairperson” means the Chairperson of the Board appointed under section 5 of the Act; and 
“Schedule” means the Schedules annexed to these rules. 


Words and expressions used in these rules and not defined herein, but defined in the Act, shall have the 
meaning as assigned to them in the Act. 


Application.— These rules shall apply to the posts as specified in column (1) of Schedule I annexed to these 
rules. 


Number of posts, classification and level in pay matrix. — The number of the said posts, their classification 
and level in the pay matrix attached thereto shall be as specified in columns (2) to (4) of the said Schedule I. 


Method of recruitment, age-limit, qualifications, etc. — The method of recruitment, age-limit, 
qualifications and other matters relating to the said posts shall be as specified in columns (5) to (13) of the said 
Schedule I. 


Disqualification. — No person, - 

who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living; or 
who, having a spouse living, has entered into or contracted a marriage with any person, 
shall be eligible for appointment to the said posts: 


Provided that the Central Government may, if satisfied that such marriage is permissible under the personal 
law applicable to such person and the other party to the marriage and that there are other grounds for so doing, 
exempt any person from the operation of this rule. 


Procedure for appointment by direct recruitment.— Where appointment by direct recruitment as specified 
under Schedule I is undertaken, the Central Government, in consultation with the Board, shall invite 
applications for the posts of officers and employees of the Board through advertisement and shall process 
appointments, either directly or through a recognised professional agency having adequate experience in 
recruitment for the Central Government, public sector undertakings or other similar bodies. 


Procedure for appointment on deputation basis—Where appointment by deputation 35 specified under 
Schedule I is undertaken, the Central Government, in consultation with the Board, shall invite applications for 
the posts through wide advertisement for appointments to posts as specified in Schedule I. 


Accommodation.— The officers and employees of the Board shall be eligible to House Rent Allowance in 
accordance with the extant rules of the Central Government as applicable to officers and employees drawing 
pay and allowances in the corresponding level in the pay matrix. 


Disciplinary proceedings.— The officers and employees of the Board shall be subject to disciplinary 
proceedings in accordance with the extant rules and regulations of the Central Government as applicable to 
officers and employees drawing pay and allowances in the corresponding level in the pay matrix. 


Conditions of service.— (1) The conditions of service of the officers and employees of the Board, in the 
matters for which no provision or insufficient provision is made in these rules, shall be governed by the extant 
rules and orders of the Central Government as applicable to officers and employees drawing pay and 
allowances in the corresponding level in the pay matrix.

12. Power to relax. — Where the Central Government is of the opinion that it is necessary or expedient so to do, 
it may, by order and for reasons to be recorded in writing, relax any of the provisions of these rules with 
respect to any class or category of persons. 


13. Saving. — Nothing in these rules shall affect reservation, relaxation of age-limit and other concessions 
required to be provided for the Scheduled Castes, the Scheduled Tribes, other backward classes, ex-servicemen 
and other special categories of persons in accordance with the orders issued by the Central Government from 
time to time in this regard.

SCHEDULE II 
[See sub-rule (4) of rule 11] 


Outdoor medical expenses. — (1) Officers and employees shall be eligible to get medical reimbursement for self 
and declared members of family. 


Explanation.— For the purpose of this clause, the expression “family” has the same meaning as assigned to it in 
the Central Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1944. 


(2) The reimbursement of outdoor medical expenses during the year shall be limited to the actual expenses or one 
month’s pay, including basic pay and dearness allowance, on 1“ January of the year whichever is lesser. 


(3) The claim should be supported by a doctor’s prescription and the original cash memos, bills for treatment by 
the doctor, purchase of medicine, and the release of increment or promotion during the year, shall not affect the 
reimbursement limit as on 1“ January. 


(4) For the officers and employees joining during the year, the annual entitlement shall be restricted on pro-rata 
basis. 


(5) The outdoor treatment shall be taken from authorised medical attendants from the panel as maintained by the 
Board. 


Indoor treatment. — (1) For the purpose of indoor treatment, the officers and employees of the Board shall be 
entitled for medical treatment at hospitals authorised by the Board in this behalf, and for this purpose, the cost of 
treatment including hospital accommodation and nursing home facility shall be as per the provisions of the 
Central Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1944 as applicable to the Central Government employees drawing 
equivalent pay. 


(2) The authorised hospitals for the purpose of paragraph (1) above shall be the same as are available to Central 
Government employees regulated by Central Services (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1944. 


(3) Treatment at authorised hospitals may be taken on the advice of the authorised medical attendants only in 
emergency.
