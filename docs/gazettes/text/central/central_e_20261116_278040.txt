सा.का.नि. 991(अ).--जबकि, केंद्रीय मोटर यान नियम, 1989 का और संशोधन करने के लिए प्रारूप नियम, 
मोटर यान अधिनियम, 1988 (1988 का 59) की धारा 212 की उपधारा (1) द्वारा यथापेक्षित, भारत सरकार के सड़क 
परिवहन और राजमार्ग मंत्रालय की अधिसूचना संख्या सा.का.नि. 664(अ), तारीख 22 जुलाई, 2026 द्वारा भारत के 
राजपत्र, असाधारण, भाग ll, खंड 3, उपखंड (i) में तारीख 24 जुलाई, 2026 को प्रकाशित किए गए थे, जिसमें उक्त 
अधिसूचना वाले राजपत्र की प्रतियां जनता को उपलब्ध कराए जाने की तारीख से तीस दिन की अवधि समाप्त होने से पूर्व, 
उससे प्रभावित होने की संभावना वाले सभी व्यक्तियों से आक्षेप और सुझाव आमंत्रित किए गए थे; 


और जबकि, उक्त राजपत्र अधिसूचना की प्रतियां जनता को 24 जुलाई, 2026 को उपलब्ध करा दी गई थीं; 


और जबकि, उक्त प्रारूप नियमों के संबंध में जनता से प्राप्त आक्षेपों और सुझावों पर केंद्रीय सरकार द्वारा विचार 
कर लिया गया है।

New Delhi, the 13th November, 2026 


G.S.R. 991(E).—Whereas, the draft rules further to amend the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 were 
published, as required under sub-section (1) of section 212 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 (59 of 1988), 
vide notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways vide number 
G.S.R 664(E), dated the 22nd July, 2026 in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part वी, Section (3), Subsection (1), 
dated 24th July, 2026 inviting objections and suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby before the 
expiry of the period of thirty days from the date on which copies of the Gazette containing the said notification were 


made available to the public; 


And, whereas, copies of the said Gazette notification were made available to the public on the 
24th July, 2026; 


And whereas, the objections and suggestions received from the public in respect of the said draft rules have 


been considered by the Central Government. 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 110 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 
(59 of 1988), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Central Motor Vehicles 
Rules, 1989, namely:- 


1. Short title and commencement — (1) These rules may be called the Central Motor Vehicles (Sixteenth 
Amendment) Rules, 2026. 


(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 


2. In the Central Motor Vehicle Rules, 1989, in rule 115, for sub-rule (7) the following sub-rules shall be substituted, 


namely:-

for this purpose by the State Government and such certificate shall be produced on demand by the officers referred to 
in sub-rule (1) of rule 116.

specified in column (3) of the Table below for the vehicle category specified in column (2):
