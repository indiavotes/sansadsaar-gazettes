MINISTRY OF RAILWAYS 
[South Western Railway (Construction Organisation)] 
NOTIFICATION 
Bengaluru, the l3th November, 2026 


S.O. 6233(E).— The Central Government, in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 20A (l) of the 
Railways, Act, 989 (24 of 989), as amended by the Railways (Amendment) Act, 2008, hereby declares its intention 
to acquire the land specified in the Schedule annexed here to for the public purpose, namely for the execution, 
maintenance, management, and operation of the special railway project, "Ballary - Hosapete 3rd & 4th line (65 Km) 
(Item#83/NR/2024-25)" in "BALLARI " district of KARNATAKA state. 


Any person interested in the said land may, within 30 days from the date of publication of this notification, 
object to the acquisition of the land for the purposes mentioned above,as provided under sub-section(l) of Section 
20A of the Railways Act, 989. 


Every objection shall be made to the competent authority, namely the Assistant Commissioner, 
Ballari,Ballari ,BALLARI ,KARNATAKA, in writing,and shall state the grounds of objection and the interest of the 
objector in the land.The competent authority shall give the object or an opportunity of being heard,either in person or 
by a legal practitioner, and may, after hearing all such objections and after making such further inquiry, if any, as it 
thinks necessary, by order, either allow or disallow the objections. 


Any order made by the competent authority under sub-section (.) of Section 20A of the Railways Act, 989 
shall be final.
