NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the |6th November, 2026 
9.0. 6287(E).— Whereas by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Housing and Urban 
Affairs, vide number S.O. 936(E), dated the 237 February, 202, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part II, section 3, sub-section (ii), dated the 26" February, 202 (hereinafter referred to as the said notification), the 
Central Government added the metro alignment of Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project, Phase-I and Phase-II in respect of 


the area of Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar to the Schedule of the Metro Railways (Construction of Works) Act, 978 (33 
of 978) (hereinafter referred to as the said Act); 


And whereas, the Central Government is of the opinion that further alteration is necessary for the construction 
and maintenance of the metro railway to which this alignment relates; 


Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-section (l) of section 32 of the said 
Act, the Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in the Schedule to the said Act to alter the 
alignment of the Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project (Phase-I and Phase-II), namely: — 


In the Schedule to the said Act, — 


(a) under the heading "Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project, Phase-I", against the entries relating to 
"Corridor-II (East - West)", for the words "Gurukul Road", the words "Shree Swaminarayan 
Gurukul" shall be substituted; 


(b) under the heading “Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project, Phase-II’, against the entries relating to 
“Corridor-I (Phase-I Extension of North-South Corridor)", for the word "JunaKoba", the word 
"Mahavirpuram" shall be substituted.
