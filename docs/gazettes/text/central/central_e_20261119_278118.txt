New Delhi, the 17th November, 2026 
SUBJECT: KHELO INDIA SPORTS GOODS MANUFACTURING SCHEME - REG. 


F. No. J-17011/276/2026-SP-V.—The Government has approved the Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing 
Scheme for the period F.Y. 2026-27 to F.Y. 2033-34 with the vision of developing India as a global hub for 
high-quality sports goods. 

2. Aligned with the Union Budget 2026-27 announcement of a dedicated initiative for sports goods 
that will promote manufacturing, research and innovation in equipment design and material science, the Scheme 
seeks to strengthen India’s sports goods manufacturing ecosystem. The growing participation in sports and 
fitness, supported by initiatives such as Khelo India and Fit India and hosting of the 2030 Centenary 
Commonwealth Games in India are expected to generate sustained demand for quality sports equipment and 
goods. NITI Aayog report “Realising the Export Potential of India’s Sports Equipment Manufacturing Sector”, 
also highlights the significant potential for expanding India’s sports goods manufacturing and export 
capabilities. The Scheme accordingly provides targeted support for investment, manufacturing capacity, 
technology, research, testing, skills and market development to strengthen the domestic sports goods 
manufacturing ecosystem and enhance its global competitiveness. 


3. The Scheme comprises eight components 
i. Support for setting up Sports Goods Manufacturing Zones 
ii. Anchor Investment Support 
iii. Turnover Linked Support 
iv. Support for Existing Clusters 
४. Testing and Certification Support 
Vi. Research and Skill Development Support 
vil. Support for Start-ups 
Vili. Branding and Outreach Support. 


4. The Scheme shall be implemented by the Department of Sports, Ministry of Youth Affairs & 
Sports, through the mechanisms prescribed herein and in the detailed operational guidelines. 


5. The Scheme shall be administered through an Approval and Monitoring Committee (AMC), headed 
by the Secretary, Department of Sports, which shall, inter alia, monitor the implementation and progress of the 
scheme. 

6. The approved outlay of the Scheme is %1,856.67 crore, comprising =1,416.67 crore for incentives, 
2415 crore for interventions and 225 crore for administration and implementation. 

7. The Scheme, as approved by the Union Cabinet, is hereby notified for implementation with 
immediate effect.

KHELO INDIA SPORTS GOODS MANUFACTURING SCHEME 


1. Vision 


The Vision of the scheme is “to establish India as a global hub for sports goods manufacturing”. 


The scheme envisions the development of an integrated and globally competitive sports goods 
manufacturing ecosystem, with focus on building manufacturing scale, strengthening domestic value chains and 
enabling manufacturers based in India to meet growing domestic and international demand. The Scheme seeks to 
create an enabling environment for sustained growth and competitiveness of the sports goods manufacturing 
sector. 


2. COMPONENTS OF THE SCHEME 


To fulfil the vision of the scheme, a comprehensive framework has been envisaged to support the 
development of a competitive and globally integrated sports goods manufacturing ecosystem. The scheme 
provides support across the sector, covering creation of new manufacturing capacity, expansion and strengthening 
of existing manufacturing capabilities, development of quality and technology capabilities, promotion of research 
and skills, support to emerging enterprises and facilitation of domestic and international market access. 
Accordingly, the scheme shall comprise the following components: 


1) Support for setting up Sports Goods Manufacturing Zones 
2) Anchor Investment Support 
3) Turnover Linked Support 
4) Support for Existing Clusters 
5) Testing and Certification Support 
6) Research and Skill Development Support 
7) Support for Start-ups 
8) Branding and Outreach Support 
2.1. ANCHOR INVESTMENT SUPPORT 


2.1.1. The Anchor Investment Support sub-component aims to catalyse significant anchor investments that 
can trigger broader industrial growth within designated zones. It focuses on encouraging enterprises to undertake 
substantial capital expenditure that contributes to value addition, creation of productive assets and establishment 
of next-generation manufacturing capacities. Through structured financial support, Anchor Investment Support 
seeks to attract large anchor units capable of building integrated supply chains, enabling ecosystem 
development, and driving long-term competitiveness and innovation across sectors. 


2.1.2. Under this subcomponent, financial support will be extended to Anchor Investors who invest a 
minimum of 100 crore inside designated SGMZs. This minimum investment requirement may be fulfilled 
either by the investor themselves or through the combined investment of the investor and their ancillary units. 
For example, an entity may set up a unit with an investment of %50 crore and bring four ancillary units investing 
12.5 crore each, thereby meeting the overall =100 crore threshold. For investors engaged in the manufacturing 
of sports equipment falling under HS Code (9506), the minimum investment requirement shall be = 50 crore. 


2.1.3. This benefit will also be available to globally and nationally renowned retail chains and National and 
global brands that bring their vendors and suppliers into the SGMZ. In such cases, the total investment made by 
all vendors associated with that retail chain / brand will be counted for determining eligibility under the 
subcomponent. Eligible up to 20% of CAPEX, 


subject to =100 crore per investor. However, in both these scenarios, the vendors or suppliers individually 
themselves will not be eligible for benefits. 


2.1.4. An additional incentive of up to 5 percentage points may be provided for cutting-edge products that 
are new to, or substantially under-deployed in, India, subject to an overall assistance ceiling of 7150 crore per 
investor under this provision. Such products may include advanced materials, embedded systems, engineered 
product designs, and advanced manufacturing processes that enhance performance, durability, safety, or user 
experience. Proposals seeking such support shall be evaluated against prescribed eligibility criteria and an 
evidence-based technical scorecard covering technology, manufacturing capability, IP/proprietary technology,

mechanism shall be considered by the AMC while determining eligibility for the additional incentive. 


2.1.5. Over the duration of the subcomponent, the units must achieve production equivalent to three times 
the value of the investment. 


2.1.6. | The sub-component shall be applicable only to those investors who commence investment within the 
first four years from the date of notification of a particular SGMZ, with production targets to be achieved over a 
period of up to four years post-investment, within the overall implementation period of the scheme. Additionally, 
not more than four Anchor Investors will be eligible for this benefit in each zones. 


2.1.7. Investors availing benefits under any Government of India existing Production Linked Incentive (PLI) 
scheme or similar schemes will not be eligible to receive incentives under this subcomponent. 


2.1.8. Units receiving the incentive must provide an undertaking to remain in commercial production for at 
least 3 years. The Anchor Investor (along with its auxiliary units or vendors, if any) is desired to achieve 100 
jobs post commencement of the production. 


2.1.9. A total outlay of 7460 crore has been earmarked for this sub-component. Support will be available till 
the earmarked funds are exhausted. 


2.2. SUPPORT FOR SETTING UP - SPORTS GOODS MANUFACTURING ZONES 


2.2.1. Under the Support for Setting up Sports Goods Manufacturing Zones (SGMZs) sub-component, 
financial support shall be provided for the development of designated Sports Goods Manufacturing Zones with 
the objective of strengthening India’s sports goods manufacturing ecosystem by enabling sports goods 
manufacturers to co-locate within a designated geography and leverage the benefits of agglomeration, shared 
infrastructure, common facilities and stronger value-chain linkages. SGMZs shall be developed through 
convergence/co-location with existing industrial infrastructure under BHAVYA and NICDC. 


2.2.2. Selection of SGMZs under the Scheme shall be undertaken through a challenge-based process, based 
on proposals submitted by eligible entities and evaluated against criteria such as availability of suitable land, 
project readiness, existing industrial ecosystem, industry participation, investment potential, employment 
generation potential, export potential and the proposed contribution to strengthening the sports goods 
manufacturing ecosystem. Initially, up to two SGMZs may be taken up in a phased manner on the 
recommendations of the Approval and Monitoring Committee (AMC), chaired by the Secretary, Department of 
Sports. Further scaling up of the SGMZ component may be considered based on 


implementation experience, demonstrated demand, performance and availability of funds Sports. 


2.2.3. Each proposed SGMZ shall preferably have a land parcel of not less than 300 acres, either within an 
existing industrial estate/industrial area or through development of a suitable greenfield zone. At least 60% of 
the land shall be earmarked for sports goods manufacturing units. The remaining area may be utilised for common 
facilities, logistics infrastructure, testing and certification facilities, worker amenities and other supporting 
infrastructure required for the development of an integrated sports goods manufacturing ecosystem. 


2.2.4. The development of the SGMZ, including industrial and common infrastructure such as trunk 
infrastructure, internal roads, utilities, industrial sheds and other park-level facilities, shall be undertaken 
through convergence with BHAVYA and NICDC, as applicable. The modalities for such convergence, 
including identification of suitable locations and funding of park-level infrastructure, shall be worked out in 
consultation with the concerned authorities. 


2.2.5. An amount of ~200 crore under the Scheme shall be utilised for the development of eligible 
infrastructure and facilities within the selected SGMZs. Such infrastructure may include facilities directly 
supporting the sports goods manufacturing value chain, such as sports goods testing and certification 
laboratories and sports product design and prototyping centres, common tooling and mould-development 
facilities, material characterisation and performance-testing facilities, demonstration facilities as well as other 
sector-specific facilities approved by the AMC. The scope of eligible sports-specific infrastructure and the 
quantum of 


support for individual SGMZs shall be determined on the basis of the approved Detailed Project Report (DPR) 
by the AMC. 


2.2.6. Financial support under the Scheme for sports-specific infrastructure shall be released in a phased 
manner against achievement of prescribed physical and financial milestones, as approved by the AMC. The 
detailed eligibility criteria, admissible components of expenditure, funding pattern, release mechanism, 
monitoring requirements and other operational modalities shall be specified in the Scheme Guidelines.

2.2.7. In addition to the SGMZs selected under this sub-component, up to two additional SGMZs may be 
notified under this provision. Eligible applicants shall be required to demarcate and delineate a land parcel of not 
less than 300 acres within their existing industrial estate for designation as an SGMZ. However, such additional 
SGMZs shall not be eligible for incentives under this sub-component. 


2.2.8. Land within an SGMZ shall be allotted only to units engaged in the manufacture of specified products 
lines under Scheme. All such allotments shall require the prior approval of the AMC and shall be subject to such 
conditions as may be specified in the Scheme Guidelines. 


2.2.9. A total financial outlay of 200 crore has been estimated under this sub-component. 


2.3. TURNOVER LINKED SUPPORT 


2.3.1. Under the Turnover Linked Support subcomponent, financial support is proposed for investors who 
commence investment within the first three years from the date of notification of the scheme. The incentive will 
be provided at the rate of 10%, 9%, 8%, 7%, and 6% of incremental turnover in consecutive years, subject to a 
cumulative ceiling of 15% of the total investment with an annual ceiling of 5% of the total investment. For 
calculating incentives, the incremental turnover in any year compared to the previous year shall be minimum of 
12%. In case of brownfield investment, incentive shall be paid on 12% increment on average turnover during the 
last 3 years; thereafter, for subsequent years, the incentives shall be paid on achieving an incremental turnover 
of 12% over the previous year. In the case of greenfield investments, minimum turnover for the first year shall be 
fixed at atleast = 0.5 crore, thereafter, for subsequent years, the incentives shall be paid on achieving an 
incremental turnover of 12% over the previous year. 


2.3.2. The incentive shall be admissible only on the net turnover of eligible products, duly verified and 
supported by certification from a Chartered Accountant based on audited financial statements and relevant sales 
records (procedural aspects shall be detailed out in guidelines). Eligible products under this category include all 
items classified under ITC (HS) code 9506. However, for investments made inside the designated SGMZ, all 
specified products lines under the scheme will also be eligible for incentives under this subcomponent. 


2.3.3. Any firm or unit investing a minimum of 10 crore (excluding land and administrative buildings) for 
the manufacturing of sports goods shall be eligible to receive benefits under this subcomponent. In addition, it is 
desired that the eligible unit to create a minimum of 30 jobs within three years after commencing operations. 


2.3.4. A total allocation of 670 crore is proposed under this subcomponent to support approximately 
150 manufacturing units expected to avail benefits under this incentive. 


2.4. SUPPORT FOR EXISTING CLUSTERS 


2.4.1. Legacy sports goods clusters such as Meerut and Jalandhar may require focused support as these 
locations evolve into mature and well-established manufacturing ecosystems. Under this sub-component, a 
comprehensive program is proposed for upgrading existing cluster infrastructure and creating common facilities 
essential for enhancing productivity and competitiveness. 


2.4.2. The total expenditure proposed under this sub-component is = 86.67 crores. The support will be 
extended subject to the condition that the respective State Governments contribute not less than 50% of the 
project cost. 


2.4.3. The interventions will be taken up based on detailed proposals submitted by the respective State 
Governments where such clusters are located. Detailed guidelines for the operationalization of this 
subcomponent will be issued once the scheme gets notified. These proposals will be examined, appraised and 
placed before the Approval and Monitoring Committee for consideration and approval. 


2.5. TESTING & CERTIFICATION SUPPORT 
2.5.1. Under the Testing & Certification Support sub-component, it is proposed to extend financial 
assistance to sports goods manufacturing companies for obtaining and renewing certifications from International 


Sports Federations. Support shall be provided up to 70% of the actual expenditure for first-time certification and 
50% of the actual expenditure for renewal. 


2.5.2. The total financial outlay proposed for this sub-component is = 105 crore.

2.6. RESEARCH & SKILL DEVELOPMENT SUPPORT 


2.6.1. Under the Research and Skill Development Support sub-component, premier educational institutions 
and research organisations shall be encouraged to establish Centres of Excellence (CoEs) within their campuses 
for undertaking research, development and innovation in sports goods manufacturing. Proposals for 
establishment of such CoEs shall be invited from eligible institutions in accordance with the prescribed 
guidelines and shall be examined and appraised by the Approval and Monitoring Committee. 


2.6.2. Co-funding of sports goods manufacturing-specific research like material science, protective 
equipments, application of infrared thermography, precision manufacturing, 3D printing etc shall also be 
undertaken under the framework of the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF). 


2.6.3. Provision of Viability Gap Funding (VGF) shall be made for the establishment of dedicated testing 
laboratories for sports goods, including facilities aligned with the standards and certification requirements of 
relevant International Sports Federations. 


2.6.4. Skill development initiatives for the sports goods manufacturing sector shall be undertaken, including 
development of training programmes, skilling interventions, and collaboration with industry and training 
institutions to create a skilled manpower base for the sector. 


2.6.5. An overall outlay of 210 crore is proposed for this sub-component, inter alia, for establishment of 
Centres of Excellence (CoEs), support under the ANRF, provision of VGF for testing laboratories, and skill 
development initiatives. 


2.7. SUPPORT FOR START-UPS 


2.7.1. The Start-up Support component shall be implemented through convergence with the existing 
DPIIT/Startup India framework, with a dedicated sports goods manufacturing priority/challenge window to 
support start-ups engaged in product innovation, design, prototyping, advanced materials, testing and validation, 
and manufacturing technologies. The Department of Sports shall, in consultation with DPIIT, identify priority 
challenge areas and facilitate participation of eligible start-ups through the existing Startup India ecosystem. 
Financial support of up to 220 lakh per eligible start-up shall be provided through the identified Startup 
India/DPIIT framework, subject to the applicable guidelines and funding modalities. The total financial outlay 
proposed for this component is = 50 crores. 


2.8. BRANDING & OUTREACH SUPPORT 


Promotion of the Indian sports goods manufacturing sector is critical for enhancing global competitiveness, 
strengthening market access, and attracting investments. The sub component provides for structured 
promotion of the sector through outreach activities, international exhibitions, and branding & PR initiatives. 
An overall budgetary outlay of =50 crore has been proposed under this component for a period of 5 years. 


2.8.1. GLOBAL SPORTS GOODS FAIRS & INTERNATIONAL EXHIBITIONS 


The sub component shall support organization of international level global sports expo in India. These 
interventions will provide a platform for domestic manufacturers to showcase capabilities, facilitate B2B 
engagements, and strengthen export linkages. 


2.8.2. OUTREACH ACTIVITIES 
The sub-component shall support outreach activities, including investor outreach, roadshows, and engagement 
through bilateral and multilateral trade platforms related to sports. BSM and RBSM-related activities may be 


aligned with, or leveraged through, the Export Promotion Mission, wherever feasible, to avoid duplication 
of efforts and enhance the overall effectiveness of export promotion initiatives.

2.8.3. BRAND INDIA CAMPAIGN 


Under this sub-component, the Department of Sports shall coordinate with the Department of Commerce to 
promote a unified ‘Brand India’ presence for Indian sports goods and equipment in international markets. A 
dedicated branding campaign may be undertaken at select global sports goods exhibitions and other high- 
visibility platforms to position India as a globally competitive hub for sports goods manufacturing. Such 
activities shall complement and not duplicate branding and marketing support available under the Export 
Promotion Mission. 


3. IDENTIFICATION OF PRODUCT LINES: 


3.1. Description of Products: 


3.1.1. All sports equipment classified under HS Code 9506, along with other items primarily intended and 
used in sports, including para sports, and physical fitness, such as racing bicycles, sports nutrition products, 
sports gear, sportswear, athleisure footwear, headgear, accessories, sports flooring, etc shall be eligible for 
benefits under the Scheme. 


3.1.2. Eligibility shall also extend to prototypes, calibration and measuring instruments, digital and display 
equipment, specialised software platforms, and other products which are predominantly used in or commonly 
associated with sports activities. 


3.1.3. The Scheme shall further cover the manufacturing of key machinery, components, inputs, and sub- 
assemblies that are primarily utilised in the production of the aforementioned sports goods. 


3.2. APPROVAL MECHANISM: 


3.2.1. In cases where any question of eligibility determination arises with respect to a particular product, 
the matter shall be placed before the Approval and Monitoring Committee (AMC) constituted under the Scheme 
for consideration and appropriate clarification. 


4. GOVERNANCE MECHANISM 


4.1. Department for Sports shall constitute an Approval and Monitoring Committee (AMC) headed by the 
Secretary, Department of Sports, will monitor the implementation and progress of the schemes and will issue 
suitable instructions and necessary guidelines from time to time. This committee will also be examining all the 
proposals and if found in order, will approve such proposals and disburse the financial support. The committee 
will include senior officials, not below the rank of Joint Secretary, from NITI Aayog, Commerce, DPIIT, CBIC, 
IFD, MSME, Concerned State Government Representative or Other Central Ministries/Autonomous 
Bodies/Agencies Representatives, 3 Industry/Academia nominees. 


The following are the key functional areas of the AMC for effective implementation and oversight of the Scheme: 
e Examine applications recommended by PMA and make appropriate decisions under the scheme 
e Approve and review the list of eligible products under target segments etc., periodically. 


e Monitor the progress of approved applicants in terms of investment, employment generation, 
production and domestic value addition 


e Monitor and review of the progress made under the scheme, including roundtable conference 
with the beneficiaries 


e Recommend amendments to scheme guidelines, including changes to target segments, 
incentive rates, etc. 


e Incase of any clarification regarding the provisions of the scheme, the decision of the AMC shall be 
final 


e In case of force majeure events, it may recommend modifications or withdrawal of specific 
scheme provisions.
